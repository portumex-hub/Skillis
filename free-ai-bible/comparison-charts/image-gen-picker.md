# Which Free Image Generator Should You Use?

## By Use Case

| Use Case | Best Tool | Why |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| Product mockups | Leonardo.ai | Photorealistic, commercial safe |
| Text in images (logos) | Ideogram | Best at rendering text accurately |
| Unlimited generation | Stable Diffusion (local) | No limits, full control |
| Quick concept art | Leonardo.ai | 150 free tokens/day |
| Video from image | Kling AI | 66 credits/day, no CC |
| Artistic/stylized | ComfyUI (local) | Full workflow control |
| State-of-the-art quality | Flux (via Replicate) | $5 free credit |

## Quality Ranking (as of April 2026)

## My Pick

For 90% of use cases: **Leonardo.ai** (free, photorealistic, no CC, 150 tokens/day)
For unlimited use: **Stable Diffusion locally via ComfyUI**
For text-heavy images: **Ideogram** (nothing else comes close)
